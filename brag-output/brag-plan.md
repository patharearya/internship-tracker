# Brag plan: Internship & Research Finder

**What it is.** A free site that gathers live internships and funded research programmes for US college students straight from employers' own job boards, USAJOBS, NSF and NIH, sorted by major, with a planner to track applying.

**For whom.** College students who otherwise check dozens of career pages to catch postings early.

**What sets it apart.** First-party sources only, no aggregators, no account, no server. Every open posting is a point in a living river on the front page.

**Most impressive claim.** 13,362 internships open right now (data/report.json, run started 2026-10-22 18:32 UTC), from 2,204 watched boards.

**Visual hook.** The river: 13,362 dots, one per real posting, coloured by major.

**Tone.** Polished, close to the site's own restrained night palette. Soft fades and dips, long enough holds to read.

**Share caption.** 13,362 internships, one dot each, read straight from employers' own job boards.

**Data note.** Posting titles and employers shown are real, public postings from data/postings.json. The planner beat reuses the README's planner screenshot, whose rows are sample stars, not anyone's personal data.
