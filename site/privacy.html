Privacy.

This site has no server and no accounts of its own. It never receives your planner, your notes or anything you type.

What is kept, and where

In your browser. The postings you star and everything in your planner (status, dates, notes, contacts, checklists, the columns you chose) are saved in this browser's local storage on your device. Nobody else can see them, including us.

In your own Google Drive, only if you sign in. Signing in with Google on the planner lets the site save the same planner as one hidden file in your Google Drive, so it follows you to other devices. The site asks Google for one permission only: to view and manage its own configuration data in your Drive. It cannot see, open or change any of your other files. The file goes straight from your browser to your Drive; it never passes through us.

Nothing else. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

What other services see

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website request, those services receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, under GitHub's privacy statement and Google's privacy policy. Signing in is handled by Google under the same policy. Internship postings link to employers' own sites, which have their own policies.

Deleting your data

From this browser: remove postings from the planner, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

From Google Drive: in Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, find Internship & Research Finder, and choose Delete hidden app data. To also remove the site's permission, go to your Google Account's third-party connections and remove it there.
